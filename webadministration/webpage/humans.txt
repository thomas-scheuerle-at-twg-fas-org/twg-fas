INITIATIVE INFORMATION:

  The Technical Working Group for Formal Automotive Systems (TWG-FAS) is an open,
  collaborative initiative to develop Vehicle Machine Language (VML), a formal
  behavioral representation standard for AI-driven automotive systems.

  Organization: Multi-stakeholder open initiative
  Founded: 2025
  Repository: https://github.com/thomas-scheuerle-at-twg-fas-org/twg-fas
  Contributors: https://github.com/thomas-scheuerle-at-twg-fas-org/twg-fas/blob/main/contributors/contributors.md

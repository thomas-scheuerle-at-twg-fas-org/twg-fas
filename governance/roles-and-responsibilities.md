# Roles and Responsibilities

This document defines participation roles and responsibilities for TWG-FAS in the founding phase.

## 1. Contributors

Contributors include any individual or organization that participates through issues, proposals, review comments, test assets, implementation work, or editorial improvements.

Contributors are expected to:

- follow the Code of Conduct
- focus on technical merit and evidence
- disclose relevant conflicts of interest
- keep discussion constructive and specific
- document assumptions when proposing normative changes

## 2. Editors

Editors maintain technical quality and internal consistency of draft documents.

Editors may:

- propose structural changes to specifications
- request clarification, references, or examples
- align terminology across documents
- classify text as normative or informative

Editors do not have veto authority over technical direction.

## 3. Maintainers

Maintainers are responsible for repository operation and publication mechanics.

Maintainers may:

- manage branch protection and merge flow
- enforce required review policy
- label and prioritize governance/process issues
- publish tagged versions and release notes
- ensure decisions are linked to repository records

Maintainers should avoid using repository control to override technical consensus.

## 4. Working Group Leads

Each active working group should have at least one lead.

Working group leads coordinate:

- agenda and meeting cadence
- issue triage within scope
- milestone planning
- consensus checks for subgroup decisions

Leads should report key outcomes to the broader project.

## 5. Founding Coordination Team

In the founding phase, a founding coordination function may perform temporary bootstrap tasks such as:

- onboarding active contributors
- creating initial working groups
- ensuring process continuity
- preparing transition to elected governance

This role is administrative and transitional, not absolute authority.

## 6. Future Elected Officers (Planned)

When participation reaches stable scale, TWG-FAS should elect officers.

Planned officer roles may include:

- Chair
- Vice Chair
- Secretary
- Release and Publication Coordinator

Election mechanics are defined during governance transition.

## 7. Role Assignment and Review

Role assignment should be transparent and based on demonstrated contribution quality and reliability.

Recommended practices:

- announce role appointments in a public channel
- provide a short role rationale
- define expected scope and review interval
- rotate roles when needed to reduce concentration risk

## 8. Role Removal

A role may be removed for inactivity, repeated process non-compliance, or Code of Conduct violations.

Removal actions should:

- include written rationale
- follow fair process
- include appeal path when applicable

# Meetings and Records

This document defines transparency and documentation practices for TWG-FAS governance and standards work.

## 1. Goals

Meeting and decision records are maintained to:

- preserve technical rationale
- support asynchronous participation
- enable auditability and institutional memory
- reduce ambiguity about past decisions

## 2. Meeting Types

TWG-FAS may run:

- Working sessions (topic-focused drafting and technical review)
- Decision sessions (consensus calls, votes, approvals)
- Coordination sessions (roadmap, release, process updates)

## 3. Agenda Practice

Whenever practical, publish agenda at least 48 hours before a planned decision session.

Agenda should include:

- objectives
- decision points
- relevant issue and pull-request links
- expected participants

## 4. Minutes Minimum Content

Meeting notes should capture:

- date and meeting type
- attendees
- topics discussed
- key arguments and unresolved points
- explicit decisions and action items
- owners and target dates

## 5. Decision Record Requirements

For each formal decision, keep a durable record containing:

- decision identifier or title
- scope
- options considered
- final outcome
- rationale
- objections and their status
- vote result (if applicable)
- links to source discussion and merged changes

## 6. Publication and Access

Records should be published in repository-accessible locations unless restricted by legal or confidentiality constraints.

If restrictions apply, publish at least a redacted summary with reason for limitation.

## 7. Naming and Location Convention

Recommended conventions:

- governance decisions: governance/decisions/YYYY-MM-DD-short-title.md
- meeting minutes: governance/minutes/YYYY-MM-DD-topic.md

These folders can be created as soon as regular meeting cadence starts.

## 8. Retention

Governance and technical records should be retained for the life of the project.

Records should not be deleted without explicit replacement and archival rationale.

## 9. Action Tracking

Action items should be tracked in issues or a shared tracker with:

- owner
- due date or milestone
- current status
- closure reference

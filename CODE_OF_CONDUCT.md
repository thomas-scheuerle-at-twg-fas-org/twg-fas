# TWG·FAS Code of Conduct

The Technical Working Group for Formal Automotive Systems (TWG·FAS) is an open and vendor-neutral initiative dedicated to the development of formal standards for automotive systems.

We believe that effective standards emerge from respectful collaboration, technical integrity, and openness to diverse perspectives. This Code of Conduct defines the behavior expected from all participants in TWG·FAS activities, including repository discussions, meetings, reviews, issue tracking, presentations, and other community interactions.

Participation in TWG·FAS implies agreement with these principles.

---

# Our Values

We strive to build a community characterized by:

* Respect
* Professionalism
* Integrity
* Technical honesty
* Openness and transparency
* Inclusiveness
* Constructive collaboration
* Scientific and engineering rigor
* Vendor neutrality

Every participant contributes to maintaining a healthy and productive environment.

---

# Expected Behavior

Participants are expected to:

## Be Respectful

Treat all participants with respect regardless of:

* employer or organization
* nationality
* background
* level of experience
* academic status
* professional role

Disagreements are inevitable in technical work. Respectful disagreement is encouraged; personal attacks are not.

## Focus on Ideas, Not Individuals

Technical discussions should evaluate:

* arguments
* evidence
* data
* engineering rationale

rather than the people presenting them.

Challenge ideas openly and constructively, but never attack colleagues, contributors, or organizations.

## Be Honest and Transparent

Communicate openly about:

* assumptions
* uncertainties
* limitations
* potential conflicts of interest

Avoid misrepresenting technical facts, sources, results, or the positions of others.

## Welcome Different Perspectives

Complex engineering challenges benefit from diverse viewpoints.
Participants should actively listen to alternative approaches and remain open to learning from others.

## Support Collaborative Problem Solving

The goal of discussion is not to "win" an argument but to improve the quality of standards and technical solutions.
Whenever possible:

* explain reasoning clearly;
* provide evidence;
* help others understand technical concepts; and
* seek common ground before escalating disagreements.

---

# Vendor Neutrality

TWG·FAS exists to develop open standards that can be implemented by multiple independent parties.

Participants should:

* act in good faith;
* avoid promoting proprietary lock-in;
* avoid using organizational influence to dominate technical decisions; and
* prioritize interoperability and long-term sustainability.

Technical merit should take precedence over organizational interests.

---

# Technical Integrity

Engineering decisions must be based on evidence and technical reasoning.

Participants should:

* acknowledge mistakes when discovered;
* correct inaccurate information;
* cite sources where appropriate; and
* distinguish clearly between facts, opinions, and speculation.

The credibility of an open standard depends on the credibility of its contributors.

---

# Inclusiveness

TWG·FAS welcomes participation from:

* individuals
* industry
* academia
* public institutions
* independent researchers
* open-source communities

We value contributions based on their quality and relevance, not on the status of the contributor.

---

# Unacceptable Behavior

The following behaviors are incompatible with TWG·FAS:

* harassment or intimidation;
* personal insults or attacks;
* discriminatory behavior;
* abusive, hostile, or threatening language;
* deliberate disruption of discussions;
* publication of private information without consent;
* knowingly spreading false technical information; and
* attempts to manipulate decisions through undue pressure or authority rather than technical reasoning.

Repeated or severe violations may result in removal from discussions, repositories, meetings, or other project activities.

---

# Conflicts of Interest

Participants should disclose conflicts of interest that could affect objectivity.
Examples include:

* commercial interests directly affected by a proposal;
* ownership of proprietary technologies relevant to a decision; and
* financial interests related to the outcome of a discussion.

Disclosure does not automatically restrict participation, but transparency helps maintain trust.

---

# Reporting Concerns

If you experience or observe behavior inconsistent with this Code of Conduct, please contact the project maintainers.

Reports will be handled confidentially and reviewed fairly and impartially.
Good-faith reports are always welcome.

---

# Our Commitment

TWG·FAS seeks to create an environment where:

* technical excellence can thrive;
* disagreements remain professional;
* contributors feel respected;
* standards are developed transparently; and
* collaboration extends across organizations, disciplines, and backgrounds.

By participating in TWG·FAS, we commit ourselves to these principles and to the long-term success of an open and trustworthy standards community.
